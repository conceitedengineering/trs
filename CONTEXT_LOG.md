## Distilled Brand Definition
- **One-liner:** The Routine Service aligns how you live and look for a more embodied life.
- **Central idea:** Soft structure: fluid + structured routines for sustainable ease and progress.
- **Method lens:** Reflection and curiosity inform action; micro-shifts compound over time.
- **Experience aim:** Help people return to themselves, not imitate someone else.

## Symbols And Motifs
- Sinks: routine, reflection, internal/external life
- Hands: touch, skincare application, craft, care
- Motion: momentum, flow, embodied action
- Recurring concepts: rhythm, consistency, duality, returning, softness, curiosity

## Audience Snapshot
- Primary: women and self-curious men, roughly 20-40
- Profile: health-conscious, high-intent, often high-earning urban but not exclusively
- Need state: wants coherence and rhythm across health, appearance, and daily systems
- Expectation: clean, elevated, aesthetically intentional delivery with practical outcomes

## Success Criteria (Strategic)
- TRS is clearly understood: what it is, what it is not, and who it serves
- Brand/content cadence feels consistent and operationally sustainable
- Offers scale toward studio + team model while preserving founder flexibility
- Website acts as a clear conversion and trust engine, not only a visual showcase

## Visual Direction Inputs
- Preferred palette zone: black/white/gray with deep tones (brown, red)
- Typography direction: thin serif tendency; still open for final pairing
- Content mix: photo + copy heavy now, with expansion to video + voice over time
- Navigation/format: refined and straightforward; editorial x educational

## Non-Negotiables And Avoids
- Avoid influencer-led tone and founder-as-product framing
- Avoid fear/deficit-heavy marketing posture
- Avoid generic, overplayed wellness language and shallow advice
- Avoid cluttered or non-intuitive navigation
- Keep abundance framing as default tone

## Offer Architecture (Business Model Context)
- **Member Space:** inspiration container and practical "how"
- **The Method:** static learning center/body literacy framework
- **The Studio:** IRL layer for high-touch services and physical transformation

## Strategic Product Boundaries
- Yes: merch and meaningful keepsakes
- No: supplement line or skincare line
- Principle: curation, filtering, and trusted guidance over product proliferation

## Working Inferences For Website (Phase 5)
- Site should feel premium and designed, while staying legible and usable
- Copy should teach and guide, not preach or perform identity signaling
- Imagery should support universal resonance and diversity, not single-person aspiration
- Squarespace feasibility should be evaluated per pattern before finalizing design direction

## Information Architecture (IA) - Working Draft
### Main navigation
- Home
- About
- Services
- Contact
- Member Space (signup + login portal)
- Studio (Phase 02, when open)

### Page intent and structure
#### Home
- Purpose: create felt orientation and a single path forward in under 5 seconds
- Evoke: immediate brand feel, curiosity, difference
- Structure: single photo/video, 1-2 lines copy, one CTA
- Notes: keep explanation off this page; use it as threshold

#### About
- Purpose: establish viewpoint, philosophy, and founder presence without over-explaining
- Evoke: understanding of TRS origin + connection to founder/practitioner
- Structure: philosophy anchor, short founder intro, why Member Space/Method

#### Services
- Purpose: bridge from orientation to offer clarity and self-selection
- Evoke: entry-point clarity, mirrored world, relief/opportunity, process confidence
- Structure: ecosystem overview + sections for Member Space, Method, Studio

#### Contact
- Purpose: direct channel for inquiry
- Structure: simple contact CTA and email

#### Member Space
- Purpose: conversion page for core container of TRS; login path for existing members
- Evoke: clear value, inner-world curiosity, conversion intent
- Structure: Member Space/Method descriptions, what's included, 2-4 testimonials, preview path
- Note: current preference is no free trial; possible preview via "Start Here" journal entry

#### Studio (Phase 02)
- Purpose: invite users into the IRL layer while reducing logistics friction
- Evoke: desire to visit, treatment understanding, ease around process
- Structure: space photos, treatment details (price/length), FAQ, address/hours
