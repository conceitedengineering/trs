# Underlying Design Philosophy (TRS)

## 1) Core Thesis
- TRS is an intimate editorial world built on soft structure.
- The experience should feel spacious, sensory, grounded, and intentional.
- The site should orient users quickly and then get out of the way.
- Premium clarity without elitism: "Prada with Apple’s accessibility."

## 2) Audience Fit + Behavioral Goals
- Primary audience: health-conscious, high-intent 20–40-year-olds seeking coherence and rhythm.
- Reduce cognitive load and decision fatigue.
- Replace generic wellness framing with precise, human clarity.
- Encourage commitment through quiet confidence, not hype.

## 3) Experience Principles
- **Soft structure:** calm, fluid, but never ambiguous.
- **Embodied clarity:** hierarchy and copy should guide felt orientation.
- **Curiosity over persuasion:** invite exploration rather than sell.
- **Slowness as a virtue:** pacing, spacing, and hierarchy should reinforce reflection.

## 4) Tone, Voice, and Language Constraints
- Voice: considered, tasteful, warm, concise, gentle, real, with light wit.
- Avoid influencer tone and founder-as-product framing.
- Avoid fear/deficit framing.
- Avoid overplayed wellness terms (e.g., "holistic," "self-care," "clean").
- Frame progress as individualized and attainable through micro-shifts.

## 5) Visual System Direction
- Palette: black/white/gray base with deep tones (brown, red) for warmth/emphasis.
- Typography: thin serif tendency, paired with a restrained utility sans.
- Visual texture: editorial, high-quality imagery, generous negative space.
- Mixed media: photo + copy heavy now, with room for video/voice later.

## 6) Symbolic Motifs
- Sinks: ritual, reflection, internal/external life.
- Hands: touch, care, application, craft.
- Motion: flow, rhythm, momentum, embodiment.
- Use as subtle cues, not literal brand clichés.

## 7) IA-Driven Page Behavior
- **Home:** threshold page. One image, one line, one CTA. No explanations.
- **About:** origin, philosophy, founder presence, and why Member Space/Method exist.
- **Services:** bridge to clarity. Explain ecosystem and allow self-selection.
- **Member Space:** conversion center. Value clarity + inclusions + testimonials.
- **Contact:** frictionless, short, direct.
- **Studio:** Phase 02. Teased but not foregrounded.

## 8) Conversion Strategy
- Primary action: Member Space signup.
- Secondary actions: Services exploration and Contact inquiry.
- Design reduces friction and builds confidence, not pressure.

## 9) Content + Imagery Strategy
- Imagery should feel universal and intimate: diversity, close crops, tactile detail.
- Avoid a single aspirational "face" or body.
- Copy should be minimal but precise, with clear reasons to stay.

## 10) Aesthetic Summary
- Editorial magazine meets educational course.
- Quiet, purposeful luxury.
- A refined system that makes the user feel seen, empowered, and grounded.

## 11) Client Comms Additions (2026-03-02)
### Global Rules
- Color use should be minimal: predominantly black and white; let media and content carry emotion.
- Scale should be petite: smaller typography overall; large type used sparingly for emphasis.
- Reduce template feel: prioritize custom styling in Squarespace to feel distinct from default plugins.
- Type preference: favor Cormorant over Moulin; brand name stays in all caps.
- Layout bias: left-justified compositions are preferred; center layouts only when clearly intentional.
- Logo presence: smaller logo scale; avoid making it a dominant visual element.

### Entry Page Rules
- No navigation; looping video with centered email capture ("Stay in the know").
