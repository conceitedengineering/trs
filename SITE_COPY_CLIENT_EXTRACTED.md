## ABOUT

## OFFERINGS
The Architecture

TRS is built around three distinct but related elements, each one addressing a different layer of the same goal: refining daily rhythm so appearance, energy, and confidence can coalesce into one.

The Member Space is the container– offering resources and inspiration.

The Method is the integration tool– providing an educational framework that guides decision-making.

The Studio produces a physical experience of touch, transformation, and product curation.

Each designed to make an impact on their own, yet work best together.

The Member Space

The Member Space is the primary entry point to The Routine Service.

It’s where people come to understand their patterns, make sense of what their body is signaling, and build a routine that feels sustainable rather than restrictive.

This is not a program with a start and end date.

It’s an environment designed to support refinement over time.

Inside, you’ll find:

Access to The Method

Weekly reorientation prompts

Journal entries, recipes, and supportive resources

Tools for working with rhythm, nourishment, and nervous system signaling

If you’re unsure where to start, start here.

The Method

The Method is the educational framework behind TRS.

It’s designed to clarify how skin, metabolism, stress response, and detox interact — and how your daily rhythm can be structured to support them.

The Method prioritizes:

Body literacy and pattern recognition

Strategic adjustments rather than overhauls

Integration into real life over optimization theatrics

Starting where you are then continuing to evolve

Science-backed methodology over influencer trends

The Method lives embedded inside the Member Space and informs all personalized work.

The Studio

The Studio is the in-person expression of The Routine Service.

All skin services are time-based and singular by nature. Each treatment is designed to meet your skin where it is that day, always built in real time rather than following a fixed protocol.

The work centers on high-touch techniques such as fascia release, lymphatic drainage, and facial sculpting, layered with advanced modalities and machines to support both immediate change and cumulative results over time.

Product curation is clean, effective, and blended intentionally. Botanical and clinical formulations are selected to support a wide range of skin types, sensitivities, and preferences, without compromising performance. I believe in harnessing the unique powers of both the cosmetic and integrative approaches to beauty because they both serve a purpose and have a point.

Sensory conscious, high-touch, results oriented– all things considered.

FAQ

Q: Is there any in-studio benefit to becoming a member?

A: Yes. Members receive preferred pricing on select products and services, along with early access to certain studio offerings as they become available. The Memberspace and studio are designed to work together—supporting continuity, orientation, and results between visits.

Q: Who is this for?

A: This space is for those looking to create steadier, more supportive routines around health, appearance, and daily life—without rigid plans or all-or-nothing approaches.

Q: How should I use the Memberspace?

A: The Memberspace is designed to be returned to. You can start anywhere, move at your own pace, and integrate what’s useful without needing to complete everything.

Q: Is this a course or a program?

A: It’s neither. The Memberspace is an ongoing resource and integration tool designed to evolve your routine into alignment. The Method is a foundational framework built to deepen body literacy, complement what you already have in place, and expand your sense of agency.

Q: What if I have questions about my personal health?

A: The Memberspace is designed as an educational and integrative resource, not a substitute for personalized medical or health care. While the content is informed by clinical experience, individual health questions are not answered within this space. For questions related to your membership or access, please reach out by email.

Stay tuned for future opportunities regarding group and one-on-one coaching.

Q: Do you offer a free trial?

A: Not at this time. The membership is designed to work over time and is best for those who have been looking for this kind of support. If it doesn’t support you as expected, you’re free to cancel at any point.
