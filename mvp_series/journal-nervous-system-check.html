Nervous System Check-In

A quick capacity check before you stack habits onto an already full day.

3 min read · Member Journal

Ask three questions: Am I wired, tired, or steady? How deep did I sleep? What feels loud in my body today?

Match your plan to capacity. High capacity supports progressive habits. Low capacity asks for simplification and recovery first.

This is how routines stay adaptive instead of rigid.
