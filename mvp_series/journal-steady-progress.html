Steady Progress & Plateaus

How to read slow progress without abandoning the routine that is working.

4 min read · Member Journal

Plateaus are often consolidation, not failure. Your system may be integrating previous changes.

Keep one anchor habit stable for two weeks while you assess sleep, digestion, and skin in parallel.

If markers are stable but appearance lags, stay with the current plan and reduce noise from new inputs.
