Patterns Before Protocol

How to observe recurring signals and build steady routines without over-correction.

6 min read · Member Journal

When routines fail, it is often because we intervene too fast. We treat a moment like a trend.

Start by naming the pattern first: what repeats across days, not just what appeared once.

From there, adjust one variable at a time and hold it long enough to learn from it.
