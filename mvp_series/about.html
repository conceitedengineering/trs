The Routine Service

The Routine Service believes in confidence that is felt, then seen.

Cultivated from years of paying close attention to people and patterns, the obvious solution became refining what already exists: natural rhythms, preferences, and lived experience.

By expanding on what is already working and reorganizing the rest, progress becomes something that can hold through change, stress, and time.

TRS weaves skin health, nourishment, daily habits, and nervous system support to create confidence that is more than skin deep.

I’m Tegan

I’m a certified health coach and licensed esthetician. My work comes from years in the treatment room and coaching clients one-on-one.

My own experience navigating skin issues led me here. It showed me how fragmented care can be and pushed me toward a more embodied, practical approach to routines for people who lead with discernment and value clarity.

I created TRS by noticing patterns, listening closely, and refining what actually holds. I knew there had to be a better way, so I built it.
