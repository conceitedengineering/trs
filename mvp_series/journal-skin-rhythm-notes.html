Skin Rhythm Notes

What changed this week, what held, and what to keep steady next.

5 min read · Member Journal

Instead of reacting to each flare, map three rhythm markers: hydration quality, stress intensity, and sleep depth.

Use simple language: calm, neutral, reactive. The trend across days is more useful than any single day snapshot.

When two markers dip at once, reduce experimentation and return to your baseline routine.
